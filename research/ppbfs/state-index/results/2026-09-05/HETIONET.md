# Hetionet external result

## Timing and selection

`MEASURED`: native labels and relationship types were preserved. H1–H4 all executed PPBFS and returned identical B0/C1 results. Five-fork screens were statistically unresolved. The predeclared H3 extension used ten paired forks and produced 0.996x `[0.954,1.041]`, with a TOST-style 90% interval `[0.962,1.032]`: `EQUIVALENT WITHIN +/-5%`.

H5 timing was excluded in full after baseline-only screening showed repeated 10–16 second cases. No C1 H5 timing was observed and no faster subset was substituted. One predetermined H5 case was retained only for occupancy measurement.

## Memory boundary

H3 d6 PROFILE memory was 8.22 MiB B0 versus 8.92 MiB C1. Both variants failed at 8/9 MiB and passed at 10 MiB and above, so the minimum observed pass was equal at 10 MiB.
